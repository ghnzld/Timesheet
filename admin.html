Backup & sync

Your live data is saved to your Claude account automatically — that's how the same numbers show up on your phone and your PC. Back up regularly into OneDrive as your permanent archive.

Back up

One click downloads everything — clients, projects, timesheet, expenses, invoices and settings — as a single dated file.

Restore

Replaces all current data with the contents of a backup file. Use this to undo a mistake or move data onto a fresh account.

Import from phone

Adds timesheet and expense entries exported from the phone app — merges them in without touching anything else. Already-imported entries are skipped automatically, so it's safe to import the same file twice.

OneDrive backup folder

Paste the web link to your OneDrive backup folder once, and the buttons above will open it directly — handy for dragging a just-downloaded file into place, or finding one to restore. A browser page can't set your download location or move files on its own, so this shortcut is the closest we can get to one click.
